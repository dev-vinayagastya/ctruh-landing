FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Ctruh?

Ctruh is the infrastructure layer for the spatial internet — a deep-tech platform that makes 3D, AR, and XR accessible to every business. Powered by a browser-native 3D engine and VersaAI, it turns images, text, and product photos into production-ready 3D assets and immersive experiences in minutes, with no installs, no SDKs, and no specialized hardware.

What is Ctruh Studio?

Ctruh Studio is an AI-powered platform for creating and managing interactive 3D experiences. Generate 3D models, manage assets, and build immersive commerce — try-ons, AR viewers, configurators, immersive stores, and AI creatives — from a single platform.

How does text-to-3D and image-to-3D work?

VersaAI, our proprietary AI engine, converts a text prompt or a single product image into a high-resolution, production-ready 3D model in about a minute — textured, optimised, and ready to deploy.

Do I need coding or 3D design skills?

No. Ctruh is no-code/low-code end to end — anyone on your team can create, customise, and publish immersive experiences with drag-and-drop tools.

Does it work on mobile, AR, and VR devices?

Yes. Experiences are WebXR-native and device-agnostic — they load in any browser from a link or QR code, with no app or SDK required.

Is Ctruh enterprise-ready?

Yes — Ctruh serves 100+ D2C and enterprise retailers with secure, scalable infrastructure and integrations into your existing systems, including Shopify and your own apps.
